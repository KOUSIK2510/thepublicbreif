The Quiet Crisis of Democratic Institutions: Why Process Matters More Than Outcome

Eleanor Ashford

Senior Political Analyst • Nov 14, 2024 • 12 min read

"We have confused the speed of decision-making with its legitimacy. The guardrails of democratic process are not obstacles—they are the very architecture of trust."

Across the developed world, a pattern has emerged that should alarm anyone who values accountable governance. The machinery of deliberation—committee hearings, public comment periods, judicial review—is increasingly treated as an impediment rather than a feature.
