About The Public Brief

Why The Public Brief
Exists

The Public Brief is more than a news platform, it is a movement to strengthen Indian journalism.

In an era of misinformation and sensationalism, we stand for journalism that is credible, authentic, and trustworthy. We believe every fact matters, every story deserves context, and every voice deserves to be heard.

By creating a platform for independent journalists, researchers, writers, and storytellers, we aim to strengthen India's journalistic landscape and ensure that truth remains at the heart of public discourse.

Strengthening Indian Journalism.  Empowering New Voices.  Serving the Public.

Founder's Message

"Without facts, there is no truth. Without truth, there is no trust."

— Maria Ressa

I founded The Public Brief with a simple purpose: to pursue the truth without hesitation and create a platform where young journalists, writers, researchers, and independent voices can share their ideas, perspectives, and stories without fear.

This is more than a news platform—it is a movement to strengthen Indian journalism through trust, credibility, and fact-based reporting. It is a place where voices are heard, facts matter, and public interest always comes first.

I invite you to be a part of this movement. Together, let us build a future where journalism serves the public with honesty, courage, and purpose.

KK

Kaifullah Khan

Founder & Editor-in-Chief
