Our Story

The Public Brief was established with one goal: to ensure that journalism is accessible, credible and meaningful. As a remote-first digital newsroom, The Public Brief has been created through the combined efforts of journalists, researchers, editors, and contributors who are united by a shared commitment to transparent, credible, impactful and factual journalism.

We believe that all serious stories should be supported by background information, different angles, and ethical reporting, rather than sensationalism. Alongside covering news, we are establishing a community where young writers and journalists can hone their skills and express their opinions.

Independent journalism is sustained by readers who support truth, openness, and accountability. Your contributions can help us enhance our reporting, promote factual journalism, provide opportunities for young writers, and maintain our freedom.

With any contribution, regardless of its size, we will be able to stay independent and focus on what is important: serving the public interest.

Thank you for your support and trust in our mission.
